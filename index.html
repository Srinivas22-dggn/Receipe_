Tasty Poha Upma Receipe

A Delicious Home Made Yummy Yummy Poha
It is Tasty and Very Nutrient Dense Food


🍽️ Ready to Eat in 40 Minutes
4 Servings Hot Hot 🔥
😋 Easy to make and tasty to eat

Ingredients Used

  • Poha of 500g
  • 4 teaspoons of mustard Oil
  • Some curry ,koriander leaves,basil leaves
  • 5 tomatoes
  • 4 onions
  • Some chilli according to your taste
  • Salt and Pepper
  • Turmeric powder,garam masala

Instructions To Make

  1. Cut the tamatoes,onions,chillies into small pieces
  2. On the stove and add the oil ,salt,pepper,turmeric powder
  3. Add the chopped onions,tamatoes,Onions and fry it for 15 minutes
  4. Take the 500g of poha and soak it in water of 2 minutes and remove water
  5. Add the garam masala to the tomatoes furry
  6. Add the poha into tomato fury and mix it well
  7. After 2 minutes turn off the stove
  8. Add Some Basil leaves into it
  9. Ready to serve!
